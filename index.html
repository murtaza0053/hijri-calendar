About

Misri / Fatimi tabular Hijri calendar (30-saal ka cycle, kabisa saal: 2, 5, 8, 10, 13, 16, 19, 21, 24, 27, 29). Hijri tareekh Maghrib ke baad badal jaati hai. Calendar logic aur miqaat list mumineencalendar.com (Murtaza Gulamali) ke basis par hai.
